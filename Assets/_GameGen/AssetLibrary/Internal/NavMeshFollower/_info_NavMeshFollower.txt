To use the NavMeshFollower, you need a NavMeshSurface in your level.
Right-click in your Hierarchy and choose AI/NavMeshSurface
Or on an Empty GameObject, at the NavMeshSurface component

On the NavMeshSurface Component, Click "Bake"

Then on the NavMeshFollower prefab, assign the player to the "Follow" field.
